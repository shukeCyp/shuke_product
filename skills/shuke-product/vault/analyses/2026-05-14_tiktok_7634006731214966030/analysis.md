    *   **钩子话术：** "If you have an O-Cedar... you're seriously missing out." (身份+好奇)
    *   **痛点话术：** "...doesn't actually get your floors clean... stuck-on grime your mop can't reach." (否定现状+指出具体问题)
    *   **承诺话术：** "...gets deep into... and that's how you get a truly clean floor." (功能+结果承诺)
    *   **转化话术：** "get 4... and 2... for just $14 with free shipping... hurry - they're selling out fast!" (优惠+稀缺+紧迫)
