# B LIFE Collagen Blend - Mexico TikTok Shop Storyboard

## Product Assumptions

- Product: B LIFE Collagen Blend, 90 capsules.
- Market: Mexico.
- Language: Spanish (Mexico).
- Category: oral beauty supplement / collagen routine.
- Core angle: daily beauty routine support, collagen habit, premium bottle, easy capsule format.
- Compliance note: avoid direct medical, anti-aging reversal, disease, or guaranteed skin-result claims. Use softer language such as "apoya", "rutina diaria", "cuidado desde adentro", and "constancia".

## Strategy

Use a Mexican UGC "amiga recommendation" structure:

1. Strong Spanish hook.
2. Show the bottle clearly within a morning routine.
3. Prove format through capsule/water/product-label shots.
4. Position as consistent daily beauty care from the inside.
5. End with TikTok Shop Mexico cart CTA.

## Character Identity Lock

Creator A: Mexican Latina woman, 32-38 years old, warm medium tan skin, oval face, natural brown eyes, shoulder-length dark brown wavy hair, soft natural makeup, small gold hoop earrings, pale pink manicure, cream satin pajama top in bathroom shots and beige knit cardigan in kitchen/table shots. Friendly big-sister UGC energy. Keep the same face, hair, makeup, earrings, manicure, and body type in every shot.

For hand-only shots, use Creator A's same medium tan hand, pale pink manicure, small gold bracelet, and gentle grip style.
