## Strategy

Make one continuous real-shot sequence:

1. Show the everyday problem after blow-drying.
2. Pick up the product from the same vanity.
3. Pump the product into the palm.
4. Apply only to mid-lengths and ends.
5. Show realistic shine and smoother ends.
6. End with a simple cart CTA.

The sequence should feel like one person filmed it in one bathroom session, not like disconnected ads.
